Sounds for read-aloud scenes and phone calls. All are public domain or CC0 (free to use, no attribution required).
Files were trimmed, made mono, loudness-matched and re-encoded to MP3; background loops were cross-faded so they repeat without a seam.
Sources: Wikimedia Commons (public domain) and OpenGameArt.org (CC0).
